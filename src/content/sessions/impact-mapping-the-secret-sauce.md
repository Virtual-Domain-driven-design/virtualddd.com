#### Details

"Impact mapping is a lightweight, collaborative planning technique for teams that want to make a big impact with software products. It is based on user interaction design, outcome-driven planning and mind mapping. Impact maps help delivery teams and stakeholders visualise roadmaps, explain how deliverables connect to user needs and communicate how user outcomes relate to higher-level organisational goals." (source: [https://www.impactmapping.org/](https://www.impactmapping.org/))

Krisztina has been teaching this technique for years in hands-on workshops, with great success, based on the feedback shared on social media. There is never enough time, though, to talk about the secret sauce that makes this method's impact long-lasting (pun intended 😉 ). Join us for a conversation about the technique and these hidden gems discovered over time, to find out why Impact Mapping is so powerful.
